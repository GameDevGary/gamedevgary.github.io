My Dev Journey

I started playing video games when I was 5 years old. The first games I remember playing are Legend of Zelda, Spyro, Crash Bandicoot, Mario, and Street Fighter. I'm 30 now, and I've played a lot of video games, and 15 years ago I decided to try to learn how to make them.

Started with googling in 2010, "how to make video games". Options at the time were licensing an expensive engine like Unreal or something, working at a company, or writing my own engine. Or use Unity and learn C#.

I googled C#, found a crash course, and taught myself programming. Downloaded Unity, started learning about 2D/3D scenes and how gameobjects and code go together and how to build them. I googled a lot of stuff about math, programming, art, computers, audio, design, story, cinematography, everything that goes into making games, I googled it. I have googled a lot.

Learning game development or any kind of art or project you create with tools and your mind is the best way to learn anything. Learning how to teach yourself is the best skill to learn for anything.

From the time I started playing video games, to the time I started making them, to now, everything I studied and learned has changed so much in ways that were always impossible to predict and keep up with what "the best way" to do anything was.
