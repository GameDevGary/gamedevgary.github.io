My Dev Journey

Like a lot of indie devs, I started out just wanting to know how game worlds were made. That curiosity turned into a massive rabbit hole of 3D modeling, texturing, and engine workflows.

I spent a long time just learning the quirks of Blender and GIMP, figuring out how to get assets looking right in Unreal Engine without artifacting. Now, I try to package up what I learn—whether that's a node setup in Blender or a full deployment pipeline for web apps like Scribbattle—so other people don't have to struggle through the same roadblocks.
